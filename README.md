## ✈️ Core Experience & Features

- 🤖 **Autonomous AI Travel Concierge:** Natural language trip planner ("I have $4000 for 10 days in Tokyo") with real-time budget breakdown, hotel recommendations, and flight routes.
- 🌐 **Interactive 3D WebGL Earth Globe:** Built with Three.js, featuring real-time country coordinate zooming, orbiting gold flight corridors, and mouse-drag rotation.
- 🛫 **Cinematic Flight Backgrounds & Supersonic Jets:** Ultra-HD photography backdrops (Sunset Wing, High-Altitude Jet Clouds, Tokyo Twilight, Alps Skyway, Maldives Seaplane) with smooth crossfade and interactive scene switcher, plus dual cruising supersonic aircraft with glowing vapor contrails and aviation beacon strobes.
- 🎟️ **Global Search Hub:** Official Trip.com booking integrations with verified partner tracking IDs (`Allianceid=10827195&SID=332665775&trip_sub3=D20047547`) across Flights, Hotels, Cruises, Car Rentals, and Activities.
- 🛂 **195-Country Visa Intelligence:** Real-time passport requirements, visa-free status, eVisa processing, and embassy assistance rules.
- 💳 **TravelTrip Travel Wallet & Virtual Titanium Card:** 0% foreign transaction fees, automated 5% cashback on travel bookings, instant multi-currency balances (USD, AED, SAR, EUR, GBP, BDT), and Apple Pay / Google Pay support.
- 📶 **Instant 5G/4G Global eSIM Store:** Instant QR delivery across 190+ countries with zero paperwork and zero roaming fees.
