Is Your Phone eSIM Compatible?

Verified device compatibility matrix updated for 2026. Search your smartphone model below.

🔒 Crucial Pre-requisite: Carrier Unlocked Device
Your handset must be carrier-unlocked (not tied to a specific operator contract).
• iPhone: Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock: "No SIM restrictions".
• Android: Settings → Network & Internet / Connections → SIMs (Status: Unlocked).
⚠️ Regional Hardware Variations:
• iPhones manufactured for mainland China (with 2 physical nano-SIM trays) do not feature eSIM microchips.
• Select Samsung Galaxy variants sold in specific Asian/carrier markets may not support eSIM. Always check your device menu for "Add eSIM / SIM Manager".
