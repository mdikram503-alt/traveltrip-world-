FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions about travel eSIMs? Here are quick answers to get you connected.

What is an eSIM and how does it work? ▼
An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM chip built directly into modern smartphones, tablets, and laptops. Instead of inserting a tiny plastic SIM card, you simply scan a QR code or tap a button to download a cellular carrier profile directly to your device.
When should I install my eSIM? ▼
You can install your eSIM prior to your trip or before boarding your flight using Wi-Fi at home or the airport. Your data plan validity will only start once the eSIM connects to a supported local network in your destination country.
Can I keep my WhatsApp and original phone number? ▼
Yes! Modern phones support Dual SIM functionality. You can keep your primary physical SIM card active for calls, SMS, WhatsApp, and 2-factor authentication bank OTPs, while routing all mobile internet data through your TravelTrip World travel eSIM.
Can I use mobile hotspot / tethering? ▼
Yes, personal hotspot and tethering are fully supported on all our data packages. You can share your internet connection with your laptop, tablet, or travel companions without any extra fee.
Which payment methods are accepted? ▼
We support Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay, and all major Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) through certified 256-bit encrypted Stripe checkout.
What if my device is not compatible or I have an issue? ▼
Check our Compatibility Guide before ordering. We offer a 100% Money-Back Guarantee if an unactivated eSIM cannot be installed or used on your device, backed by our 24/7 live WhatsApp support.
