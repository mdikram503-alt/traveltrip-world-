Refund Policy

Clear, Fair, and Customer-First Protection

1. Full Refund Eligibility (14-Day Guarantee)

You are entitled to a 100% full refund within 14 days of purchase under the following verified conditions:
• The eSIM profile cannot be provisioned or activated due to technical errors on our system or the wholesale carrier network.
• You arrive at the designated destination and the eSIM fails to register on supported partner roaming networks, provided you contact our support team to troubleshoot and 0 MB (zero data) has been consumed.
• You inadvertently purchased a duplicate identical order and report it prior to activation.

2. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds cannot be granted under the following circumstances:
• Carrier-Locked Devices: Your smartphone is locked to a specific carrier (e.g., AT&T, SoftBank, Vodafone) preventing third-party eSIM profile installation.
• Device Incompatibility: Your device model does not feature native eSIM hardware capability (such as regional dual physical-SIM iPhone models sold in mainland China).
• Data Consumed: Once an eSIM has successfully connected and data traffic has been transmitted, the package is non-refundable.
• Change of Travel Plans: If you cancel your trip after the eSIM has been generated, but choose not to use it before expiration.

3. Refund Processing Procedure

To request a refund, message our 24/7 WhatsApp support at +971 52 441 3931 or email support@traveltrip.world with your Order ID (e.g. TT-CA4B959C) and screenshots of any network error screen. Verified refunds are credited directly to your original payment method (PayPal or Credit Card) within 3 to 5 business days.
