Browse destinations How to activate

1. Choose a country

Pick a local, regional, or global plan. Prices follow the currency in the header.

2. Pay on this store

Pay securely on Traveler checkout. You get a QR and an activation string.

3. Add eSIM on the phone

iPhone: Settings → Cellular → Add eSIM. Android: Network → SIMs → Add eSIM. Turn roaming on.

iPhone XR and later · Pixel 3 and later · Galaxy S20 and later · see the full model list. Unlocked phones only.
