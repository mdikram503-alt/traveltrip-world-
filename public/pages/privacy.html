Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Committed to Customer Confidentiality

1. Information We Collect

When you order an eSIM or register an account, we collect:
• Contact details (Name and Email address for eSIM delivery and account access)
• Technical metadata (IP address, device compatibility model, and browser type for fraud prevention)
• Order history (Package purchased, transaction reference, ICCID, and activation status)
We do not collect or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank login details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is strictly used for:
• Provisioning your international eSIM profile via wholesale carrier APIs
• Delivering your QR code, LPA installation string, and order invoice
• Providing responsive customer support via WhatsApp or email
• Authenticating your customer portal and preventing unauthorized order snooping

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:
• Payment Gateways (PayPal / Stripe): To securely process payments under PCI-DSS compliance.
• Wholesale eSIM Providers (ResellPortal): To generate your GSMA-compliant SIM profile and ICCID.

4. Data Security & Retention

All communication is encrypted via TLS 1.3/HTTPS. Passwords in our database are salted and hashed using cryptographic SHA-256. Order lookups require mutual matching of both secret Order ID and buyer email to prevent unauthorized data access.

5. Customer Rights & Inquiries

You have the right to request a copy of your stored order data or ask for account deletion at any time. For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact our Data Protection representative at support@traveltrip.world or WhatsApp +971 52 441 3931.
