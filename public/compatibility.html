📱 Universal eSIM Compatibility

IS YOUR DEVICE COMPATIBLE?

Almost all smartphones manufactured after 2019 support digital eSIMs.
Search your model below or check your settings in 10 seconds.

🍎

Apple iPhone / iPad

Go to Settings > Cellular (or Mobile Data). If you see "Add eSIM" or "Add Cellular Plan", your iPhone is ready!

🤖

Samsung Galaxy

Go to Settings > Connections > SIM Manager. If you see "Add eSIM", your phone is 100% compatible.

🌐

Google Pixel & Others

Go to Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs. Tap "+" or "Download a SIM instead?" to verify.
