Is Your Phone eSIM Compatible?

Most modern smartphones manufactured after 2019 support digital eSIM profiles.

⚠️ Important Requirement: Carrier Unlocked Device
Your device must not be carrier-locked to a specific telecom network (e.g. AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone contract lock). You can verify this in: iPhone: Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock: "No SIM restrictions".
