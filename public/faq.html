Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about traveling with TravelTrip eSIM.

1. What is an eSIM and how does it work?
An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital microchip built directly into your smartphone. Rather than inserting a physical plastic card, you simply scan our QR code to download an international data plan onto your device.
2. When does my plan validity start?
Your plan validity countdown typically begins the moment your device first connects to a supported cellular network at your destination country. We recommend installing the eSIM before departure and enabling Data Roaming upon arrival.
3. Can I keep my existing WhatsApp number and physical SIM?
Yes, 100%! You can keep your primary physical SIM active for standard incoming calls and SMS, and your WhatsApp account will continue using your normal phone number seamlessly while using TravelTrip for high-speed travel data.
4. Can I use personal hotspot and tethering?
Personal hotspot and tethering are supported on the vast majority of our regional and global eSIM plans, subject to local roaming network specifications.
5. What should I do if the QR code won't scan?
Every purchase includes a manual LPA activation string (e.g. LPA:1$rsp.esimaccess.com$...). You can copy and paste this code directly into your phone settings without scanning.
6. What is your refund policy?
If an eSIM fails to activate or connect due to verified technical errors on the supplier network and no data has been consumed, we provide a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Simply reach out to support with your Order ID.
