How TravelTrip eSIM Works

An eSIM is a digital SIM card built into your phone. Follow this step-by-step guide to get connected in under 2 minutes.

1

Connect to Wi-Fi

Make sure your iPhone is connected to an active Wi-Fi network (at home, airport, or hotel) before scanning.

2

Open Camera & Scan QR Code

Go to Settings → Cellular (or Mobile Data) → Add eSIM.
Select Use QR Code and point your camera at the QR code shown on your TravelTrip account screen or order confirmation.

3

Turn On Data Roaming at Destination

Label your new plan as Travel. When you land at your destination airport, set Cellular Data to your TravelTrip eSIM and switch Data Roaming: ON. You're online!

Connect to Wi-Fi

Go to SIM Manager & Add eSIM
