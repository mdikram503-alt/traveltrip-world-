Land. Scan. You’re online.

Traveler eSIM is the travel-data shop of Traveler, a telecommunications company in Ajman, United Arab Emirates. Owned by Mohammad Akram. Buy a plan for the country you are flying to, pay in your currency, install with a QR code. No plastic SIM. No airport kiosk. Your home number stays on your original line.

Browse destinationsHow to activate
1. Choose a country

Pick a local, regional, or global plan. Prices follow the currency in the header.

2. Pay on this store

Card or Google Pay on Traveler checkout. You get a QR and an activation string.

3. Add eSIM on the phone

iPhone: Settings → Cellular → Add eSIM. Android: Network → SIMs → Add eSIM. Turn roaming on.

iPhone XR and later · Pixel 3 and later · Galaxy S20 and later · see the full model list. Unlocked phones only.
